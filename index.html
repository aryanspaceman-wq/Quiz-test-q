<h2>Why this study matters</h2>

<p>This project supports UN Sustainable Development Goal 12: Responsible Consumption and Production. We look for associations between energy drink habits and things like age, area, friends, social media, routines and awareness. We do not assume any one of them causes consumption, and the results will not claim to represent all teenagers in Qatar.</p>
